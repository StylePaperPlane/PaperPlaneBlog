# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors reading a personal technical blog, reverse-engineering and security peers exploring the author's work, and the owner maintaining the site through the administration interface.

## Product Purpose

PaperPlane presents the author's articles, projects, music, and personal identity in one coherent site. Success means visitors can read comfortably, understand who the author is, and reach the author's configured social profiles without the presentation obscuring the site's character artwork.

## Brand Personality

Soft, technical, personal. The interface should feel calm and authored, with the established blue and pink palette supporting the content and background artwork.

## Anti-references

Generic dashboard templates, floating card stacks, pill-shaped controls used as decoration, heavy shadows, ornamental glass panels, placeholder social links, and responsive layouts that crop the main subject out of hero artwork.

## Design Principles

- Let the artwork and writing carry the page; interface elements should not compete with either.
- Treat saved settings as a public contract: configured values appear consistently, empty values disappear completely.
- Art-direct responsive imagery around its subject instead of applying a centered crop at every width.
- Preserve clear hierarchy with spacing and alignment, not decorative containers.
- Keep feature logic in focused modules so homepage and about-page behavior cannot drift apart.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, meaningful Chinese accessible names, reduced-motion behavior, and usable layouts from 390px mobile viewports through large desktop screens.
